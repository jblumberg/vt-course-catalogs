Course Title: French 2

Grade Levels: 10-12

Course Length: 1 year

Prerequisites:  Completion of French 1 with a 2.9 or higher

Course Description: This course allows the student to further explore the French language and culture.  Short stories, dialogues, online tools and authentic materials still provide the basis for student exploration.  The student continues to develop more complex speech patterns.  Emphasis continues to be on developing strategies to understand the basic meaning of authentic materials.  All French courses are discussion-based classes and verbal participation is mandatory.

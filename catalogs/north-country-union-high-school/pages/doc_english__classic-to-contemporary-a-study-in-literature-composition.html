Course Title: Exploring the Northeast Kingdom: The Works of Howard Frank Mosher

Grade Levels: 11-12

Course Length: Semester

Prerequisites:  No

Course Description: This course dives into the rich, evocative world of Howard Frank Mosher’s Where the Rivers Flow North and its accompanying short stories. Set in Vermont’s rugged Northeast Kingdom, Mosher’s works explore themes of resilience, cultural identity, environmental change, and the passage of time. Students will analyze the literary elements of Mosher’s stories, including his vivid use of setting, character development, and exploration of human connections to the natural world.  Through close reading, creative projects, and analytical writing, students will gain a deep appreciation for Mosher’s storytelling and the way it reflects the spirit and struggles of rural New England life.

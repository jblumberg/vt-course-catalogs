Course Title Introduction to Anatomy & Physiology

Grade Levels 11-12

Course Length: Semester

Prerequisites Must have successfully completed Biology or teacher recommendation

Course Description 

This rigorous one semester course provides exploratory experiences, laboratory, and real-life applications to better understand the human body.  Topics to be covered will include a review of basic chemistry, cells and tissue with an emphasis placed on the interrelatedness of such systems as the skeletal, muscular, digestive, nervous and circulatory.  This course has a substantial laboratory component including a fetal pig dissection.

Course Title:  Lifetime Activities for Women

Grade Levels: 9-12

Course Length: semester

Prerequisites: None 

Course Description: This course introduces students to a series of hobbies and activities that appeal to a wide range of mature age groups.  Students learn the value of an active lifestyle and how lifetime pursuits support physical, mental and emotional health.  Social skills developed through the course are cross curricular and benefit students in the classroom, workplace and beyond.  Units include both indoor and outdoor activities such as disc golf, pickleball, bowling, volleyball, yard games, geocaching, walking/hiking, canoeing/kayaking, biking, fishing, archery, snowshoeing, cross country skiing, sledding, ice skating and other leisure activities.  Emphasis will be on participation, attitude and willingness to try new things in a variety of environments.  This is a P.E. class for students who enjoy the outdoors and like moderate-paced activities that focus on individual performance and setting personal goals for improvement.

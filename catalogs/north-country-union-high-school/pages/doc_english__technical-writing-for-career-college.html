Course Title: Technical Writing for Career & College

Grade Levels: 11-12

Course Length: Semester

Prerequisite: No

Course Description: This course is an English Language Arts class for any student in grade 11 or 12 who wants to develop their technical writing skills for either career or college.  This course will focus on writing techniques and styles that are crucial for success in both career and college.  Students will focus on developing the types of reading, writing, and grammar skills that are necessary in becoming effective communicators.  Areas of study will include: research papers, report writing, research and design reports, memos, emails, proposals, technical descriptions of equipment and procedures, and progress and feasibility reports.  Students will also familiarize themselves with reading technical documents and manuals as well as preparing and presenting oral reports including the effective use of graphics.

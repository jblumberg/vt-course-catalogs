Course Title Government & Economics 10

Grade Levels 10

Course Length: 1 year

Prerequisites

Course Description 

This course is a study in US government, politics, and economics. The Constitution and other founding documents will serve as the basis of study with units in the Founding Era, the three branches of federal government, civil liberties and civil rights, American ideologies and beliefs, political participation, and the basics of economics. This course satisfies one of the three years of social studies required for graduation.

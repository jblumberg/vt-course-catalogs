Course Title  Weather & Climate

Grade Levels  10-12

Course Length: Semester

Prerequisites N/A

Course Description 

This course will delve into the fascinating world of weather and climate, investigating the factors that contribute to various weather events and exploring the differences between weather and climate. Through engaging lessons and hands-on activities, students will learn about the processes that shape our atmosphere and gain a deeper understanding of how and why different weather patterns occur. From studying the formation of clouds and precipitation to examining the impact of global climate change, students will develop a comprehensive understanding of the intricate interplay between the Earth's atmosphere and our daily weather experiences. Join us on this exciting journey as we unravel the mysteries of weather and climate!

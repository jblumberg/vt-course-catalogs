Course Title: Community Service Learning

Grade Levels: 9-12

Course Length: Plan based

Prerequisites: None

Course Description:

Community Service Learning is an educational opportunity that combines learning goals and community service in ways that can enhance both student growth and the common good.  It is a teaching and learning strategy that integrates meaningful community service with instruction and reflection to enrich the learning experience, teach civic responsibility, and strengthen communities.

Course Title: Dual Enrollment

Grade Levels: 11-12

Course Length: Semester

Prerequisites: Dependent on course choice

Course Description:

This program serves to introduce students to college-level coursework before they complete high school applies college coursework to secondary school graduation requirements, and enables students to graduate from high school with a jumpstart on college.  The program allows for two tuition-free college courses for eligible Vermont high school students. Note that under this program the tuition is fully covered. Students and their families are responsible for the cost of any fees and books/materials associated with the course. The Vermont State Dual Enrollment Program is a college-level course that becomes part of the student’s transcript, regardless of where the class is taught.  Dual enrollment credits will be considered and accepted by most state colleges as transfer credits. Core credit may be awarded also. These courses are offered during the school year as well as summer. Students must be referred to the Flexible Pathways Coordinator to initiate and complete this process.

Course Title: Early College

Grade Levels: 12

Course Length: Year

Prerequisites: Academic good standing junior year

Course Description:

Students who will achieve their senior status may complete their senior year at a state college or community college.  Students may have the opportunity to live on campus (requires payment of room and board) or students can commute to the school on their own.  Students who wish to play sports are eligible as long as they do not participate at the college level.  Students should contact the Flexible Pathways Coordinator for further details.

Course Title: Expanded Learning Opportunities

Grade Levels 9-12

Course Length: Plan based

Prerequisites: None

Course Description:

The Expanded Learning Opportunities pathway recognizes that learning can take place in any aspect of a person’s life.  Expanded Learning Opportunities include but are not limited to Internships, Extracurricular activities (sports, clubs & theater), independent studies, educational summer camps, Governor’s Institute of Vermont, volunteering, and more.  Opportunities exist to earn credit in a variety of areas.  This opportunity is provided for students who have the ability to function independently and who wish to learn about an area that is not offered in the regularly offered courses and programs.  Students should contact the Flexible Pathways Coordinator for further details.

Course Title: Mentor Program

Grade Levels: 9-12

Course Length: Plan based

Prerequisites: None

Course Description:

Mentors work one-on-one or with kindergarten(ers) through sixth graders at one of the local elementary schools in a classroom setting or an educational experience.  Together, typically, they spend time playing a game, reading a book, talking, helping with class work, or getting to know one another.  Mentors provide a role model for the mentee and give them the supplemental attention that can make a difference in their school day.

Course Title  Work-Based Learning

Grade Levels 9-12

Course Length: N/A

Prerequisites None

Course Description: Students can gain credit through experiential learning in the workplace.  Students will design a learning plan and gather evidence of transferable skills while building community relationships in a work placement.  Work Based Learning involves short-term unpaid experiences such as job shadowing, career exploration, internships, and long-term paid positions.  There is also an Online Career Exploration Course available for students who would like to explore their career and college opportunities and lessons in 21st-century job skills.  Students should contact the Work-Based Learning Coordinator for further details.

Course Title  Professional Pursuits

Grade Levels  Open to gr. 10-11

Course Length: Semester

Prerequisites  None

Course Description

This course is designed to support students in developing their professional skills to prepare them for engaging in the workforce.  It offers opportunities for students to build a professional portfolio through resume and cover letter writing and additional resources for documenting their skills and experience.  Additionally, students will learn how to search for jobs, interview effectively, communicate professionally both in person and virtually, and explore post-secondary college and career options.  This course will provide the opportunity to meet industry professionals to learn about career opportunities in our community and throughout the State of Vermont.  The course is semester long and focuses on the following transferable skills:  clear communication, self-direction, problem-solving, and responsible citizenship.

Course Title: Dance 2

Grade Levels: 10-12

Course Length: one semester

Prerequisites: two semesters of Dance 1

Course Description:

This course is designed for students that have already taken a full year (two semesters) of Dance 1.  Students will be challenged with more difficult combinations and instruction in individual body control, strength, and stamina.  We will learn the proper technique in a variety of styles and students will have the opportunity to begin creating their own dance phrases and choreography related to specific styles.

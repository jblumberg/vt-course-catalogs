Course Title Beginning Piano

Grade Levels 9-12

Course Length: One Semester

Prerequisites None

Course Description 

Beginning Piano is designed to provide students with fundamental piano skills. Students will learn basic music skills and piano techniques. Classroom time will be spent learning to read music, play scales, while also learning traditional melodies and chords. Recommended for beginners, students with minimal training, or students who play “by ear” and would like to learn to read music. Prior musical background is not necessary. Enrollment limit is 10.

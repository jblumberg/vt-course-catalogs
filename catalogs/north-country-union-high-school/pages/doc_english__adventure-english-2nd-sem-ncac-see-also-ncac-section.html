Course Title:  Adventure English-2nd Sem. (NCAC)  (see also NCAC section)

Grade Levels: 11-12

Course Length:  Semester 2

Prerequisites: Because of safety concerns, students must be skilled in self-direction, working in a group or team setting, following both verbal and written directions and have a high level of integrity or truthfulness. Students must also possess a level of physical fitness and perseverance that allows them access to the field-based curriculum delivered in the remotest regions of the Northeast Kingdom amidst extremely harsh weather.  

Course Description: This course is outdoor oriented with most days spent outside in all types of weather. Writing assignments take place atop the tallest mountains and looking out over the best vistas of the Northeast Kingdom. Reading assignments often take place in hardwood forests. This course runs in tandem with Adventure PE, a physically demanding outdoor PE course. The reading material provides the learner a level of insight into the mind and psyche of the world’s greatest explorers and adventurers that can only be gleaned by reading firsthand accounts and well-constructed retellings by the world’s greatest writers of nonfiction.  Read about adventures to the world’s highest peaks, its frozen wastelands, its deserts, and its vast oceans.  Read what kills even the most skilled and bravest adventurer.  Read what it takes to survive.  In addition to these texts, students read magazine articles published by the leaders in exploration written by the most skilled authors of our time.  Students learn to record, journal, and construct written accounts of their own experiences and encounters gained during their enrollment in the Adventure PE Course – a high-level canoeing, kayaking, mountain biking, and backcountry skiing and snowboarding physical education course for juniors and seniors enrolled in either the Career Center or the High School.

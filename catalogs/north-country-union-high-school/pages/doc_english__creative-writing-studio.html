Course Title: Creative Writing Studio

Grade Levels: 11-12

Course Length: Semester

Prerequisites: No

Course Description: This course introduces students to writing in a studio environment.  Students are given the opportunity to develop and refine effective expression of ideas and to gain further practice in various forms of writing.  Participants learn to develop an awareness of language and to develop an individual writing style.  Writing skills are strengthened by learning how to develop ideas in logical, convincing, and interesting ways.  Students work with a process, which involves generating ideas, planning, drafting, giving and receiving constructive feedback, revising, and proofreading.  Regular opportunities to share their writing are included in the schedule.

Course Title Chemistry

Grade Levels 10-12

Course Length: Year (semester based)

Prerequisites Must have successfully completed or be concurrently enrolled in Algebra 1.

Course Description

This course will run as two, semester-long courses to offer flexibility in student scheduling.  Students signing up for Chemistry should sign up for both semesters when registering for classes.  Chemistry has been referred to as the “central science” because of its relationship to all science disciplines.  This course is a logical third year science course for almost all high school students.  Students will build on their prior science knowledge by investigating physical and chemical properties of matter as well as physical and chemical change in the chemistry lab and how change is symbolized in chemistry.  Atomic Theory and Atomic structure will be explored with emphasis on how atomic structure is reflected in the Periodic Table of elements.  Students will engage in chemistry lab work and will use writing skills to produce lab reports.

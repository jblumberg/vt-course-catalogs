Course Title: Taylor Swift’s Storytelling 

Grade Levels: 11-12

Course Length: Semester

Prerequisites: No

Course Description: Storytelling is at the heart of human connection, weaving together shared experiences and individual journeys. This course explores the power of narrative through the lens of music and poetry, focusing on the works of Taylor Swift as a contemporary storyteller. Students will listen to, read, and analyze her lyrics as literary texts, uncovering themes, poetic language, and figurative devices that echo timeless human emotions and connect to works by renowned poets and authors.

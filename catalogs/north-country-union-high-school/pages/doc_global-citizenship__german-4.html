Course Title: German 4

Grade Levels: 12

Course Length: 1 year

Prerequisites: Students must complete German 3 with a proficiency of 2.9 or higher.

Course Description: This course is designed to review skills already learned, to enrich vocabulary, to expose the student to greater authentic language in a variety of contexts and from a variety of sources, and to increase their effectiveness in communicating in real life situations.  Students explore and discuss issues using authentic resources.

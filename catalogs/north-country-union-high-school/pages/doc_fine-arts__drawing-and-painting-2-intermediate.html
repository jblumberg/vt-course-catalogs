Course Title:  Drawing and Painting 2- Intermediate

Grade Levels: 10-12

Course Length: 1 year

Prerequisites: Successful completion of Drawing and Painting 1- Introduction  (or Advanced Art in Middle School with Portfolio Review/ teacher approval prior to start of course)

Course Description: This course is designed to develop the students' drawing and painting skills through an in-depth study of a variety of media and techniques such as: pencil, charcoal, pen and ink, pastel, oil pastel, watercolor, tempera and acrylic paint.  Career exploration, art appreciation, artistic criticism and aesthetic analysis will be included.  Emphasis will be placed on acquiring drawing and painting skills and encouraging creative growth in a variety of media. This course builds on the knowledge and skills learned in Drawing & Painting- Intro.

Course Title History of Vermont

Grade Levels 10-12

Course Length: 1 semester

Prerequisites Students wishing to enroll need to have completed World History 9 or have proper teacher recommendation.

Course Description 

Did you know Vermont was the location of America’s first large Naval Battle?  Did you know that Vermont considered being a part of Canada or a separate country prior to joining the United States?  Or, that for a brief period of time, Vermont was the largest wool producer in the world?  Learn about the history of where YOU live by taking History of Vermont:  People and Places.  We will study The Green Mountain State’s people, including indigenous Abenaki peoples, famous founders like Ethan Allen, trailblazer Alexander Twilight, to some of the more obscure like Wilson “Snowflake” Bentley.  We will also study the history of Vermont’s famous mountains, trees, and farms along with what it is about Vermont which has inspired locals and attracted flatlanders for generations!

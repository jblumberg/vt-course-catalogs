Course Title:  Printmaking

Grade Levels: 9-12

Course Length: .5 year

Prerequisites:  successful completion Drawing & Painting- Intro  

Course Description: This course is designed for students to gain experience in printmaking techniques.  Utilizing skills learned in Intro. to Drawing & Painting, students will create prints using linoleum block, wood blocks, collagraphs, dry point, and silkscreen (serigraphy).  Paper/Textile printing will be in black & white and color.  A large amount of sketch work will be required.  Emphasis will be placed on mastering technique and materials while developing artistic growth and creativity.

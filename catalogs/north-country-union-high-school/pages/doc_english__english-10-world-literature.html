Course Title:  English 10 World Literature

Grade Levels: 10

Course Length:  Year-Long

Prerequisites:  No

Course Description: This course supports the continued acquisition of reading and writing skills.  Students will explore world literature from the perspective of the different genres.  This course will broaden their background in reading and aid them in writing about or responding to a literary selection.  In addition to Scott Foresman’s World Literature anthology, major works to be studied may include, but are not limited to: Before We Were Free, Sold, Things Fall Apart, Arthurian Legends, and a work by Shakespeare.

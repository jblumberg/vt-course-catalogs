Course Title Genocide & the Holocaust:  An Interdisciplinary Exploration

Grade Levels 11-12

Course Length: 1 semester

Prerequisites  Students wishing to enroll in this elective must be a 11th- 12th grader with at least 1 history credit

Course Description 

Students will use a project/inquiry based approach to study the concept of “genocide”. Through infamous episodes in history such as the Nazi Holocaust, the Rwandan Genocide, and the Darfurian Genocide, students will use frameworks from history, political science, literary and film critiques, psychology, sociology, philosophy, and religious studies to explore the conditions present which allowed for such atrocities to take place. Students will also explore the lasting impacts these atrocities have left on the world today and develop conclusions on how best to prevent future genocides.  In general, each unit will call upon students to engage in classroom discourse with their peers.

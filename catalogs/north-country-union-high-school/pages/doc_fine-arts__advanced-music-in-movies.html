Course Title Advanced Music in Movies

Grade Levels 10-12

Course Length: One Semester

Prerequisites This class is by teacher recommendation ONLY. Students must take Music in Movies before taking this course.

Course Description 

This course will be offered 2025-2026.  This course is designed for students who have shown an interest and dedication towards studying music in movies.  This course is a more advanced examination of film scores.  Students will be expected to gain a stronger understanding of musical concepts as well as cinematic techniques.  Students will be REQUIRED to participate in class discussions as well as present for the class.  Students will get to choose some of the films that will be watched in class. This course is offered every other year.

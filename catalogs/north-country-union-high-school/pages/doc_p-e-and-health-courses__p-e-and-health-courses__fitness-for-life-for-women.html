Course Title: Fitness for Life for Women

Grade Levels: 9-12

Course Length: semester

Prerequisites: None 

Course Description: This course is designed for the purpose of introducing female students to fitness in a more comfortable environment.  Students will be educated in a variety of areas including but not limited to, cardiovascular fitness, strength training, balance and agility.  Students will also learn the roles that nutrition and rest play in their overall wellness.  Students are required to create and keep a journal recording their progress.

Course Title AP Environmental Science

Grade Levels Open to grades   11-12 

Course Length: Year

Prerequisites Must have successfully completed Algebra 1 and Chemistry or teacher recommendation.

Course Description 

This course will be offered 2026-2027.  Advanced Placement Environmental Science is an elective one credit course.  This course is designed to be the equivalent of a college level environmental science course.  APES covers a wide expanse of material relating to our natural world, human world, and how the world is changing.  Topics covered include ecosystems, biodiversity, populations, earth systems and resources, land and water use, energy resources and consumption, atmospheric pollution, aquatic and terrestrial pollution and global change.

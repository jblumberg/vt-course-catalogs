Course Title:  Adv. Teamwork

Grade Levels: 11-12

Course Length: semester

Prerequisites: None

Course Description: This course is designed to expose students to high intensity cardio exercise in primarily a highly competitive team sport setting.  Emphasis throughout the course will be placed on team sports that students can pursue as adults within an hour drive of the Northeast Kingdom.  Activities will include volleyball, ice hockey, tennis, pickleball, lacrosse, rugby/Australian football, ultimate Frisbee, amongst a few.  The goal for students completing this course is to improve, and maintain, cardiovascular health while developing a foundation of skills for life-long participation in team sports in and around the Northeast Kingdom.

Course Title: Ceramics & Sculpture 1-Intro

Grade Levels 9-12

Course Length: 1 Semester

Prerequisites  None

Course Description:

This semester course is suited for the beginning art student.  Students will receive a foundational understanding of additive and reductive sculpture, packaging construction, as well as clay processes, tool identification, and a wide variety of sculptural and ceramic construction techniques.  Students will have plentiful opportunities to practice creative expression and develop their skills at creating, connecting, responding, and presenting artwork.  Students are expected to assess and reflect on their artwork.  Assessments will be largely based on students’ engagement level with the work.

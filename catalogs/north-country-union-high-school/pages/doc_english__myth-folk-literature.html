Course Title: Myth & Folk Literature

Grade Levels:  11-12

Course Length: Semester

Prerequisites: No

Course Description: Why do people tell stories?  Why do communities tell myths and legends?  Are fairy tales an important part of childhood development?  What are the connections between story and identity?  This course explores the vital role that storytelling plays in our lives, and how it shapes our sense of who we are.  Students will explore creation and hero myths from around the world, as well as classic fairy tales.  Students are also introduced to the pantheon of Greek and Norse gods and their associated stories.  The course also examines the oral, literary, cultural, folkloric, historical, psychological, archetypal, and philosophical components of folk tales

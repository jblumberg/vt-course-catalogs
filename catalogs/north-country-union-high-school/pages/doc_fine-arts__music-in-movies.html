Course Title Music in Movies

Grade Levels 9-12

Course Length: One Semester

Prerequisites None

Course Description 

This course is designed for students who love movies and want to explore the ways that music impacts the creation and viewing experience of films. The course will include learning key concepts about both music and films, watching movies with notable scores/soundtracks, and discussing the connections between the music and the audience response. While some musical terms will be taught, this is NOT a music theory class and will focus mostly on the films themselves. Students will have a direct impact on the movies chosen for each class. The course could include such films as Jurassic Park, Harry Potter, Black Panther, Coco, Star Wars, and more. Students can only take this course one time.

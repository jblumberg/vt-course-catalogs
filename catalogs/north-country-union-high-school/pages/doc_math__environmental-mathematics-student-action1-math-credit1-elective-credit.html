Course Title: Environmental Geometry & Student Action(1 math credit/1 elective credit)

Grade Levels: 10-12

Course Length: Full Year, 2 blocks

Prerequisites: Successful completion of Algebra 1

Course Description: This course will require two blocks (3 & 4 - with M/B TBD) In this class, you will learn how to collect environmental data in the woods, on farmland, and in wetlands, which you will then use to produce maps that help us to understand the landscape. You will learn how to analyze data in Google Sheets and ArcGIS, which is a map making software. Additionally, we will use our maps and data analysis to do environmental service work such as tree planting, river restoration, and invasive species control. Partners such as the Memphremagog Watershed Association, Northwoods Stewardship Center, and VT Fish and Wildlife will help us with our work. At the end of the course, you will have a portfolio of work that you can share in college applications and/or with future employers. If you have been looking for "real-world" math, this is the course for you.

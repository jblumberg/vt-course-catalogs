Course Title:  Professional Pursuits

Grade Levels:  Open to gr. 10-11

Course Length: Semester

Prerequisites: None

Course Description: This course is designed to support students in developing their professional skills to prepare them for engaging in the workforce.  It offers opportunities for students to build a professional portfolio through resume and cover letter writing and additional resources for documenting their skills and experience.  Additionally, students will learn how to search for jobs, interview effectively, communicate professionally both in person and virtually, and explore post-secondary college and career options.  This course will provide the opportunity to meet industry professionals to learn about career opportunities in our community and throughout the State of Vermont.  The course is semester long and focuses on the following transferable skills:  clear communication, self-direction, problem-solving, and responsible citizenship.

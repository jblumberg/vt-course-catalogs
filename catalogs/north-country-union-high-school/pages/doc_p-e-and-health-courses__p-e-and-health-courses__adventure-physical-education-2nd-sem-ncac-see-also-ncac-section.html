Course Title: Adventure Physical Education-2nd Sem. (NCAC) (see also NCAC section)

Grade Levels: 11-12

Course Length: Semester

Prerequisites: Students must already be an intermediate skier/snowboarder and try out during Outdoor Club outings.

Course Description: This course provides high-level backcountry skiing and snowboarding education to juniors and seniors already experienced in alpine descent.  Students are expected to climb mountains in the winter months.  Experienced instructors provide training in off-piste skills, avalanche education, and mountaineering.  Instructors use mountain climbing, backcountry skiing and snowboarding combined with adventure travel to develop skills in leadership, problem-solving, communication, risk management, and teamwork.  This program is designed for the adventurous skier and snowboarder who is interested in taking multi-day trips to explore the highest peaks in the Northeast.  The course runs in tandem with Adventure English to create a two-block experience entitled the North Country Adventure Course.

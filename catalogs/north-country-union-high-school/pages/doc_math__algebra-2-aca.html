Course Title Algebra 2 (ACA)                   (see also in ACA section)

Grade Levels 10-11

Course Length: Full Year

Prerequisites successful completion of Algebra 1 and Geometry.

Course Description 

Algebra 2 through the Arts & Communications Academy is a course that combines the scope and sequence of the traditional Algebra 2 curriculum with the context of real-world arts and communications applications.  Students who have been accepted in the Arts and Communications Academy will be given first enrollment priority.

Course Title Government & Economics 10 (ACA)

Grade Levels 10

Course Length: 1 year

Prerequisites 

Course Description

Using a thematic approach with an arts focus, this Social Studies and Economics course addresses the forming of the United States Government and the Constitution itself as well as themes related to the Constitution such as law and current political debates.  This class also focuses on micro and macro economics.  Other work includes vocabulary study, literature, grammar review, and writing of all types as well as connections chronicled in the visual and performing arts.  This course is designed for any 10th grade student who has successfully completed a 9th grade Social Studies course.  Students who have been accepted in the Arts and Communications Academy will be given first enrollment priority.

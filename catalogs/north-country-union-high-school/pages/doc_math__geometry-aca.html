Course Title Geometry (ACA)                         (see also in ACA section)

Grade Levels 10-12

Course Length: Full Year

Prerequisites successful completion of Algebra 1.

Course Description 

Geometry through the Arts & Communications Academy is a course that combines the scope and sequence of the traditional geometry curriculum with the context of real-world arts and communications applications.  Students who have been accepted in the Arts and Communications Academy will be given first enrollment priority.

Course Title: French 3

Grade Levels:  11-12

Course Length: 1 year

Prerequisites: Students completing French 2 with a proficiency score of 2.9 or higher can move to French 3.  All students who have taken a school year off from French, must pass a test with the French instructor for prior approval to enroll in a French 3 or higher course.

Course Description: Students are expected to use primarily French in class.  Short-stories,  IGCSE/DELF A1/A2 tasks (listening, reading, speaking and writing) continue to provide the basic material.  Grammar exploration is more detailed.  Students develop the skills necessary to give and defend opinions and to elaborate on their answers.  Communicative activities help the student develop clarity and organization in speaking and writing.  The purchase of a French-English dictionary is strongly recommended.  All French courses are discussion-based classes and verbal participation is mandatory.

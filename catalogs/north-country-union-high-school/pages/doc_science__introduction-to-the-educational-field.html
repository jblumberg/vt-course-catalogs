Course Title Introduction to the Educational Field

Grade Levels 10-12

Course Length: Semester

Prerequisites none

Course Description

Students will be introduced to fundamental topics of prek-12 education including educational/developmental psychology, major historical shifts in education, an exploration of student and teacher mindsets and teaching techniques.  Students will be encouraged to develop their own philosophy of education, and identify career options that may be of interest to them in the education field.  As they study these topics, students will develop communication, collaboration, and independent research skills necessary to be successful in future education coursework.

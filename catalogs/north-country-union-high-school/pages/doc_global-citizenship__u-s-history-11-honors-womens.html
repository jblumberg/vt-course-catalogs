Course Title U.S. History 11 Honors

Grade Levels 11

Course Length: 2 semester

Prerequisites 

Course Description 

The chronological sequence of this course will begin with the emergence of the United States as a world power at the turn of the 20th century and will continue to the present. The focus is on how domestic and foreign policies and events have shaped the modern US. The major themes of this course will focus on migration, urbanization, imperialism, civil rights, the changing role of government, and the evolving role of the US in global affairs. Through a variety of historical thinking skills, compelling questions, and various modes of presentation, students are expected to use this knowledge to better understand the related international involvements and national interests. Students should expect to have demand for work completion outside of class time.

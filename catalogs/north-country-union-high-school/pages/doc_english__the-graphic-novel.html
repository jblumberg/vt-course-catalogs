Course Title: The Graphic Novel

Grade Levels: 11-12

Course Length: Semester

Prerequisites:  No

Course Description: This course will explore the genre of graphic novels as a cultural and artistic process.  The social, metaphorical, and philosophical meanings/themes in graphic novels will be analyzed and discussed.  Beginning with Art Spiegelman’s classic interpretation of the Holocaust, Maus, students will read from and write about a variety of works using the comic book form.  Other books will include Fahrenheit 451, They Called Me Enemy, and Pride of Baghdad. In addition to analyzing both the form and the content of graphic novels, students will produce a piece of graphic storytelling that deals with one or more contemporary social issues.

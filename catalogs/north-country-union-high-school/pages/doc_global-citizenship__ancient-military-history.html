Course Title: Ancient Military History

Grade Levels: 10-12 only

Course Length: 1 semester

Prerequisites:  None

Course Description: This course will be offered 2026-2027.  Students will explore pivotal battles during ancient World History.  Through an inquiry approach focused on individual battles and eras, Ancient Military History will include not only important military tactics, technologies, and leadership but also political, historical, and social impacts these battles and military leaders had, and continue to have, on the world.

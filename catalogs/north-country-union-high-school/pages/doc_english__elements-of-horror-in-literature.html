Course Title: Elements of Horror in Literature

Grade Levels: 11-12

Course Length: Semester

Prerequisites: None

Course Description: Elements of Horror in Literature focuses on the genre of horror and monster lore. Students will study the dark side of literature through an in-depth exploration of a variety of horror stories and novels. From classics such as Dracula and Macbeth to contemporary novels such as The Girl with All the Gifts, students will explore how the earliest gothic works are still influencing horror writers today. Students will also learn about the origins, lore, and creative imaginations that surround such things as zombies, vampires, ghosts, madness, death, and the things that go bump in the night.  This course is reading and discussion intensive. Students will read 40-50 pages between classes and engage in discussions that will challenge perceptions and ignite the imagination. They will dissect the complexities of these novels and short stories, unraveling hidden meanings, and connecting the dots between modern fears and those of the past. There will be several analytical papers/projects and a formal research paper. The course final is to write and shoot a short horror film.  **Several works will contain adult themes, including violence + disturbing images, mild language, and adrenaline-inducing terror.

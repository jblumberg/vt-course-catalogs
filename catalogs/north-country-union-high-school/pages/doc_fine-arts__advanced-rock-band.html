Course Title Advanced Rock Band

Grade Levels 9-12

Course Length:  1 semester

Prerequisites Audition required. Rock band class preferred but not mandatory

Course Description 

Advanced Rock Band is a performance-based class intended to teach students how rock music is learned, rehearsed, and performed.  Students will work together to learn how to sing and play more advanced rock music.  This advanced music will include some difficult techniques and concepts such as soloing, tapping, odd times, guitar harmonies, background vocals, stage presence, and more.  Technology will be used to research songs, record music, and help learn instruments.  The class will include at least one live performance.  This is an auditioned class – guitar/bass/keyboard students must be able to read music OR tablature at a basic level, be able to play through some songs, have basic correct technique, and show willingness to play a wide variety of music. All instruments (saxes, trumpets, etc.) are welcome to audition for this class but will have the same type of audition.

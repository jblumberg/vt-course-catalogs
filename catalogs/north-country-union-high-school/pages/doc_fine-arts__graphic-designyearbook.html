Course Title: Graphic Design/Yearbook

Grade Levels 9-12

Course Length: Full Year

Prerequisites: Drawing and Painting 1-Intro

Course Description: 

This year-long class will teach the art of graphic design from the standpoint of creating the school yearbook and other projects for press. Students will use the on-line program through TreeRing, as well as the Adobe Creative Suite (InDesign, Photoshop, Illustrator) to create the school annual and other projects. Students will plan and design around a theme for the falcon yearbook, in addition to learning about package design. Students will also learn about company ID and branding. Students will study design history, as well as the science of printed design. Students who complete all of their assignments, receive a yearbook at the end of the year for their efforts. The course may be taken a semester at a time with permission from the instructor.

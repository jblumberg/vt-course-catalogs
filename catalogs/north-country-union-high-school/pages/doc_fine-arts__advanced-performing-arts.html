Course Title: Advanced Performing Arts

Grade Levels: 9-12

Course Length: Full Year

Prerequisites: by audition or teacher permission

Course Description: 

This course is designed to offer the advanced performing arts student an opportunity to continue personal exploration and growth.  Actors, singers, dancers, instrumentalists, and technical theater students are welcome to audition.  The class includes members of the Select Choir, Jazz Band, and advanced Dance & Theatre students.  Attendance during the school day and at scheduled rehearsals and performances is mandatory. This class may be repeated. There is a Physical Education or Fine Arts credit option. Students MUST audition to be a part of this class and can only be added once they receive teacher approval.

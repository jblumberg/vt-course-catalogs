Course Title Financial Math 2

Grade Levels 12

Course Length: Semester

Prerequisites this course is for 12th grade students only

Course Description 

This spring semester course explores financial topics around taxes, credit, and budgeting.  Learn how to manage day-to-day finances and make decisions as a financially responsible adult.  Projects include completing federal and state income taxes.  Students can choose between completing their real taxes or a tax simulation using an online program.  This course can be taken separately or combined with Financial Math 1.

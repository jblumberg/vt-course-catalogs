Course Title: AP German Language and Culture

Grade Levels: 12

Course Length: 1 year

Prerequisites: Students must complete German 3 with a proficiency of 2.9 or higher and get teacher recommendation, as well as complete a summer assignment.

Course Description: This course is designed for students who wish to take the AP German Language Examination.  It is for advanced students who want to develop German language skills including the ability to understand spoken German in formal and conversational situations, the development of vocabulary skills for reading newspaper and magazine articles, contemporary literature and other non-technical writings, and an ability to express ideas accurately and resourcefully.  Students are expected to take the AP test.

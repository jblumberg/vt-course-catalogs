Course Title: Contemporary Voices in Literature and Composition

Grade Levels: 11-12

Course Length:  Year-Long

Prerequisites:  No

Course Description: This course explores major themes in contemporary novels and non-fiction texts and encourages students to understand more than one perspective. Students will read, discuss, and analyze literary selections. They will examine authors’ techniques and will gain awareness of how literature reflects society. Students will read and write about a variety of works including literature from different cultures, authors, and societies.  The course will help students become more culturally literate and globally aware while developing vocabulary, reading comprehension, and composition skills.

Course Title Govern. & Economics 10 Honors (ACA)

Grade Levels 10

Course Length: 1 year

Prerequisites 

Course Description 

Using a thematic approach with an arts focus, this Social Studies and Economics course addresses various themes found in the forming of the United States Government and the Constitution itself as well as themes related to the Constitution such as law and current political debates.  This class also focuses on micro and macro-economics.  Other work includes vocabulary study, literature, grammar review, and writing of all types as well as connections chronicled in the visual and performing arts.  This course will include additional writing and reading assignments outside of class.  Writing assignments concentrate on essays of all types as well as the research paper.

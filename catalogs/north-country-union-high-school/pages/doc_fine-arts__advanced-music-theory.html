Course Title Advanced Music Theory

Grade Levels 10-12

Course Length: Full Year

Prerequisites At least two years of experience in band, chorus, or both

Course Description 

This course is offered every other year and will be offered 2026-2027. This course will spend a brief period of time reviewing basic reading and notating skills before moving on to more rigorous and challenging material.  This course will also cover sight singing, ear training, composition, musical analysis, and an introduction to a large variety of repertoire.  This course is designed for students intending to pursue music as a career or  passionate musicians interested in expanding their musical literacy.  Students may receive college credit for this course.

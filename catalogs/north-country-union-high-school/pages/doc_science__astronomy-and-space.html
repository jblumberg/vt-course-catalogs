Course Title Astronomy and Space

Grade Levels 11-12

Course Length: Semester

Prerequisites Must have completed Earth and Space Science and Algebra.

Course Description 

Topics and activities in this class are focused on comparing the conditions, properties, and motions of objects in space with Earth.  In addition, concepts, theories, and principles that are basic to Earth will be studied to help students in their understanding of the materials, processes and/or phenomena that directly or indirectly affect Earth.  In addition, students will explore past, current, and future space explorations and missions to compare the technology that helps advance our knowledge and understanding of the space beyond Earth and other parts of the Universe.  As scientific learners, students will conduct research, collect and analyze data, interpret results and draw conclusions.

Course Title: Health Education

Grade Levels: 9-12

Course Length: semester

Prerequisites: None

Course Description: This course will cover a variety of health topics such as:  stress management, depression/suicide awareness, communication and advocacy skills, building a community of tolerance and respect, substance use/misuse, nutrition and exercise, sexuality, and health literacy.  By the end of this course, students will be able to identify their individual risk factors, protective factors, and community assets.  Students will also learn how to apply healthy problem-solving skills that can be applied in various situations.  National and local data help drive this course when discussing the prevalence of these issues in our community.  This course is required for graduation.

Course Title: Ceramics & Sculpture 2 - Intermediate

Grade Levels: 9-12

Course Length: Half year

Prerequisites: Ceramics and Sculpture 1-Intro

Course Description 

This course is designed to further develop 3-D perceptions, manipulative skills, craft quality and artistry through an in-depth study of ceramic and sculptural media.  Building on the knowledge and skills from C&S1, students will be required to visualize, design, and construct functional and decorative pieces.  Emphasis will be on hand building methods and pottery wheel. Students are expected to assess and reflect on their artwork. Teacher’s assessments will be based on the student's engagement level and quality of design and construction. Students are expected to be self-motivated and utilize class time and materials wisely. We are a team when it comes to studio maintenance and all students are expected to help clean the studio.

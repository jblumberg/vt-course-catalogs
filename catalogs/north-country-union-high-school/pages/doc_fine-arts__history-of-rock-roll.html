Course Title History of Rock & Roll

Grade Levels 9-12

Course Length: semester

Prerequisites none

Course Description 

History of Rock & Roll is a semester class that is open to any student, regardless of prior musical background.  The curriculum will cover the origins and evolution of rock music and its relationship to society.  Beginning in the early 20th century with the roots of blues music, the course will cover rock history up to 1990.  Active class participation and classwork are the most important elements of the curriculum.

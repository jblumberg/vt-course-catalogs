Course Title:  Our Stories: The Power of Memoir Writing

Grade Levels: 11-12

Course Length: Semester

Prerequisites:  No

Course Description: In this engaging and thought-provoking English class, students will delve into the world of memoirs, a literary genre that blends personal reflection with storytelling. Through a variety of memoirs written by authors from diverse backgrounds, students will explore themes such as identity, memory, family, culture, and resilience. Throughout the semester, students will analyze different memoirs, examining the narrative techniques, voice, and structure used to convey the author’s personal journey. Discussions will focus on understanding the unique power of memoirs to reveal deeper truths about the human experience. Students will also engage in writing their own memoirs, reflecting on significant moments in their lives, and will learn how to craft compelling, authentic narratives. By the end of the course, students will gain a deeper appreciation for the art of memoir writing and an enhanced ability to connect personal experience with broader themes in literature.

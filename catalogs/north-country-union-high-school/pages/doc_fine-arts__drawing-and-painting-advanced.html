Course Title:  Drawing and Painting 3- Advanced

Grade Levels: 11-12

Course Length: 1 year

Prerequisites:  successful completion of Drawing & Painting- Intermediate  with a “B” or better or permission of instructor.

Course Description: This course is designed for the advanced art student who wishes to acquire proficiency in all drawing and painting media and methods.  Emphasis will be placed on developing the student’s personal artistic style and improving critical thinking skills such as:  analysis, inference, interpretation and evaluation.  Focus will be on visual communication, individual creativity, aesthetic awareness and career choices.  All students are expected to enter their work in artistic exhibitions and competitions. Students will participate in the Student Art Exhibits and curate art shows on their own.

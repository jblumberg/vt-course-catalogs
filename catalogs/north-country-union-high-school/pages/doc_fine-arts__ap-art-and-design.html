Course Title:  AP Art and Design

Grade Levels: 11-12

Course Length: 1 year

Prerequisites:  Must have completed Drawing & Painting 2-Intermediate.

Course Description: This course is similar in content to Art Studio, however, students receive Advanced Placement credits by enrolling in the Advanced Placement Art & Design (drawing, two-dimensional design and three-dimensional design portfolio).  Students enrolling in the Advanced Placement course(s) will be expected to submit a portfolio to the AP College Board for review and assessment.  It is recommended that students have already taken an Art Studio section with a focus on an AP portfolio.

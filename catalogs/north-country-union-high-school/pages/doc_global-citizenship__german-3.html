Course Title: German 3

Grade Levels: 11-12

Course Length: 1 year

Prerequisites: Students must complete German 2 with a proficiency of 2.9 or higher.

Course Description:  This course provides the opportunity for the student to use all the skills s/he has mastered to successfully communicate in realistic situations.  Students explore and discuss issues of global interest using authentic materials.  The study of voice and idiomatic language continues.  Grammar is embedded in course content.

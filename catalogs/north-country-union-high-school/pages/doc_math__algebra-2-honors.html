Course Title Algebra 2 Honors

Grade Levels  10-12

Course Length: Full Year

Prerequisites successful completion of Algebra 1 and Geometry and teacher recommendation. (Successful completion is a 3.0 or better in both courses)

Course Description 

Algebra 2 builds on the skills learned in Algebra 1 and Geometry. The study of functions continues with polynomial, exponential, and additional functions. The honors sections of this course will cover additional Algebra 2 topics including logarithmic functions and rational functions.

Course Title Spycraft

Grade Levels 11-12

Course Length: 1 year

Prerequisites Government and Economics or AP Government and Politics

Course Description

Spycraft is an applied civics/national security studies course that provides a basic and broad overview of the history of espionage, the authorized activities of an intelligence professional, the composition of the United States Government Intelligence Community (IC), the various functions of each of the member agencies, the limits and capabilities of American spy agencies, and how Intelligence plays a role in the decision-making process of the federal government. This course is also designed to apply critical analysis to the field of national security studies by examining key topical issues of interest to Intelligence studies such as terrorism, public health, failed states, great powers conflict, transnational crime, and cybersecurity.

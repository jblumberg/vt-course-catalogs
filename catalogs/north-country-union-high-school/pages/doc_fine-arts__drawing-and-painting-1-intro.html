Course Title: Drawing and Painting 1- Intro

Grade Levels 9-12

Course Length: 1 Semester

Prerequisites : None

Course Description: 

This is an exploratory course for the beginning art student.  It will introduce the student to a variety of two-dimensional media and various drawing and painting techniques. Content to be covered includes an introduction to the elements and principles of art, art appreciation and aesthetic judgment.  Emphasis will be placed on the student’s personal creative and artistic growth as well as developing the student’s artistic criticism and analysis skills.

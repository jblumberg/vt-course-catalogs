Course Title AP U.S. History

Grade Levels 11*-12

Course Length: 1 year

Prerequisites  a full year of U.S. History.  *Gr. 11 students must have teacher permission.

Course Description  

This course will be offered 2025-2026. The AP U.S. History course focuses on the development of historical thinking skills (chronological reasoning, comparing and contextualizing, crafting historical arguments using historical evidence, and interpreting and synthesizing historical narrative) and the development of students’ abilities to think conceptually about U.S. history from approximately 1491 to the present. Seven themes of equal importance – American and National Identity; Migration and Settlement; Politics and Power; Work, Exchange, and Technology; America in the World; Geography and the environment; and Culture and Society – provide areas of historical inquiry for investigation throughout the course. These require students to reason historically about continuity and change over time and make comparisons among various historical developments in different times and places. Students should have advanced skills in analysis, synthesis, contextualization, and argumentation.

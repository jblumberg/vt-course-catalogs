Course Title: Contemporary World Literature Honors

Grade Levels: 11-12

Course Length: Year-Long

Prerequisites: No

Course Description: More than ever we live in a connected global world, and recent world events demonstrate the necessity of cooperation between and understanding of diverse cultures.  This course will facilitate that knowledge with contemporary literature and film study from around the world, looking at specific political and cultural conditions as well as the human qualities that are common to all people.  Students will engage with current trends through research, presentations, written analysis, and discussion.  Texts to be studied include, but are not limited to, People of the Book, Angela’s Ashes, Like Water for Chocolate, and A Long Way Gone.  Students must be able to read and write fluently and independently in order to be successful in this class.

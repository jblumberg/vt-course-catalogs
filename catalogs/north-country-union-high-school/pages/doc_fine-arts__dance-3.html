Course Title: Dance 3

Grade Levels: 11-12

Course Length: one semester

Prerequisites: two semesters of Dance 1 AND two semesters of Dance 2 ***Students may also ask for the instructor’s permission to be in Dance 2 or 3 if they feel that Dance 1 or 2 will not be challenging enough for them.

Course Description:

This course is designed for students that have taken a full year of Dance 1 AND a full year of Dance 2.  Students will be challenged with advanced combinations and choreography and instruction in individual body control, strength, and stamina.  Students will begin creating their own dance phrases and choreography related to specific styles and have the opportunity to teach different styles to their peers.  This class also focuses largely on refining technique as dancers.

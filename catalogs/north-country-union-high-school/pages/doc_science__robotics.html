Course Title Robotics

Grade Levels 11-12

Course Length: First Semester

Prerequisites N/A

Course Description 

This course is the first semester only due to VEX Robotics competitions in Dec. & Jan.  This rigorous course continues the investigation of exploratory experiences, laboratory and real life applications to better understand the branches of engineering using VEX Robotics.  Robotic building and programming is the major component of the course work.  This course is intended for students who are interested in robotics and are willing to travel at least two Saturdays for VEX Robotics competitions in December and January.

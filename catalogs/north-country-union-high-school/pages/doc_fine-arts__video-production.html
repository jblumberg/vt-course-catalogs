Course Title: Video Production

Grade Levels: 9-12

Course Length: Two Semesters

Prerequisites None

Course Description:

Students will explore the art of movie making in the digital age. Students will learn how to tell a story and create an artful movie using IMovie. They will also study film-making techniques and materials.  Students will become familiar with cameras, editing, and all other aspects of project management from pre-production to finish. The course will also take a look at film history and how the unique language of film developed. Through readings, instructive videos, as well as watching examples by famous directors, students will develop their own video vocabulary, and practice these skills in the creation of  their own movies.

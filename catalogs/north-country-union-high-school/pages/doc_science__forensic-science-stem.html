Course Title Forensic Science (STEM)

Grade Levels 11-12

Course Length: Semester

Prerequisites Must have successfully completed 2 years of science.

Course Description 

This STEM course is designed to offer a learning experience that integrates the concepts learned in previous science courses, and to strengthen individual skills in scientific reasoning and observation.  Students will learn basic forensic science terms, techniques and skills that are necessary in gathering, processing, and analyzing crime scene evidence.  The goal of this course is for students to gain a more meaningful understanding of how the methods and principles of forensic science are applied in a crime scene investigation.

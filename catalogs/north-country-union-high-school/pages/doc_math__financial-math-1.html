Course Title Financial Math 1

Grade Levels 12

Course Length: Full Year

Prerequisites this course is for 12th grade students only

Course Description

This course explores financial topics around banking and investing.  Pay yourself first and learn how your money can grow in value to help create a secure financial future.  Various investment types are explored so that students can weigh risk and rewards when making financial decisions.  Students will understand the basics of banking including online banking.  Projects include a stock market simulation and exploring the power of compound interest. This course can be taken separately or combined with Fin. Math 2.

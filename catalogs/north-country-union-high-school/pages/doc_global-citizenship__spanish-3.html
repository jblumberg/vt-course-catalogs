Course Title: Spanish 3

Grade Levels: 11-12

Course Length: year

Prerequisites: Students completing in good standing, Spanish 2.

Course Description: Students are expected to use primarily Spanish in class. Storytelling and reading continue to provide the basic material. Grammar exploration is more detailed. Students develop the skills necessary to give and defend opinions and to elaborate on their answers. Communicative activities help the student develop clarity and organization in

speaking and writing.

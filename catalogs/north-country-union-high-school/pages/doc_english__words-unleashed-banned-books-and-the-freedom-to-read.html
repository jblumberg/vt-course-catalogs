Course Title:  Words Unleashed: Banned Books and the Freedom to Read

Grade Levels: 11-12

Course Length:  Semester

Prerequisites:  No

Course Description: Step into the captivating world of literature that has sparked controversy, challenged societal norms and pushed the boundaries of expression. "Words Unleashed" is a thought-provoking course that delves into the pages of books that have been banned or challenged throughout history. This course invites students to critically examine the reasons behind censorship, explore the power of storytelling, and engage in meaningful discussions about freedom of expression.

Throughout the semester, students will embark on a literary journey that spans genres, cultures, and time periods. From classic novels to contemporary works, the course will feature a diverse selection of banned or challenged books, offering students the opportunity to understand the socio-political context surrounding each banned text.  *** This course is READING and discussion intensive. **

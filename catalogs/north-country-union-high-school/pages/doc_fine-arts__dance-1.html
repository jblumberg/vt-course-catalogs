Course Title: Dance 1

Grade Levels: 9-12

Course Length: one semester

Prerequisites: This course is meant for students that have never taken a dance class at North Country before.  It will most likely include freshmen/sophomores.

Course Description:

This course introduces students to dance by engaging with the multiple purposes of the art form through hands-on experiences and by investigating dance as it relates to other artistic disciplines.  We will explore elements of a wide variety of dance styles and practices through movement explorations, dance-making, readings, discussions, videos, written responses, collaborative projects, and attendance at live dance events.  By examining dance in both global and local contexts, we will move towards a fuller appreciation and understanding of the influence of dance in our everyday lives as well as an embodied reflection and expression of the world in which we live.

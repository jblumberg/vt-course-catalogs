Course Title World History 9

Grade Levels 9

Course Length: 1 year

Prerequisites placement determined by Grade 8 teacher recommendation

Course Description 

This course focuses on the major trends, events, and ideas that have shaped the world from ancient times to the present.  Students will analyze major questions including the following:  How has geography shaped the course of human history?  Why do empires rise and fall?  How do we know what we know? What makes a revolution successful?  Does history move in a predictable pattern or not? What is the ideal society?  How much has changed over time?  Through analysis of evidence (especially primary sources), argument development, inquiry, and looking at history through multiple perspectives, students will gain valuable critical thinking skills and have a better understanding of their place in the world.

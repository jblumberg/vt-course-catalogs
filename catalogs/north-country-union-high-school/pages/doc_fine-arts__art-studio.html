Course Title:  Art Studio

Grade Levels: 10-12

Course Length: Year

Prerequisites:  Must have completed Drawing & Painting 2-Intermediate.

Course Description: This program is an intensive independent study course designed for the serious, motivated art student who wishes to pursue his/her interest in art at a higher level and intends to major or minor in visual arts after graduation. It requires a highly motivated, artistically advanced, dedicated student who is willing to work hard to improve his/her artistic skills and talent.  Students enrolled in the program for two years are expected to complete an art portfolio by the end of their first year and improve and update it in their second year.  Students will be expected to spend a minimum of ten hours a week working independently outside of the classroom.  Focus for the two years will be on admission to fine arts schools or universities and emphasis will be placed on development of personal artistic growth and a development of artistic style and self-awareness.

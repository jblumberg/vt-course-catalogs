Course Title Science and Technology (TKC)

Grade Levels 9-10

Course Length: Year

Prerequisites enrollment in the Kingdom Course

Course Description 

The science portion of TKC uses the outdoors as our classroom. We will focus on the four pillars of the environment : Forestry, Wildlife, Water, and Soils. We will also study Vermont Natural History. We often work with professionals in the field including wildlife biologists, fisheries biologists, and foresters. Students should be prepared for hands-on learning in the field as well as analyzing data in the classroom.

Course Title:  American Song Lyrics and Poetry

Grade Levels: 11-12

Course Length:  Semester

Prerequisites:  No

Course Description: This course focuses on the genre of poetry and song lyrics.  Students will read, listen to, analyze, discuss, and write about American poems and songs. Both songs and poetry are artistic expressions that reflect the diversity of American culture and Americans themselves.  Common themes such as the American Dream and the conflict between the individual and the community are explored.  Through a variety of important voices, this class ultimately asks students to reflect on their learning and discover for themselves what it means to be an American, how that perception may differ from those around them, and how understanding this can lead to unity and progress.  Some commonly taught authors and artists include: Edgar Allan Poe, Taylor Swift, Walt Whitman, Emily Dickinson, Tupac Shakur, Langston Hughes, Maya Angelou, Robert Frost, and Katy Perry.  Students will study poetic language, figurative language, and theme.  The culminating project, a research presentation or paper, will be based on a chosen song or poem selected by each student.

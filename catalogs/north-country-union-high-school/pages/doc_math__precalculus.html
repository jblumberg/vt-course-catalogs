Course Title Precalculus

Grade Levels 10-12

Course Length: Full Year

Prerequisites successful completion of Algebra 2 or teacher recommendation.

Course Description 

The course begins with the study of polynomial functions, zeros, the rational root theorem, inequalities, the properties of functions, and their applications.  Logarithmic and exponential functions are covered including solving logarithmic equations and applications.  A substantial trigonometric unit covers trigonometric functions, identities, addition formulas, and applications.  This unit also includes solving trigonometric equations and right triangle trigonometry.  The year concludes with a unit on sequences and series and an introduction to limits.

Course Title:  Leaders in Literature

Grade Levels:  11-12

Course Length:  Semester

Prerequisites: Stipulation: Freshman and Sophomores taking this course must also be enrolled in a year-long English course

Course Description: We see leadership in our everyday lives through our interactions and experiences with peers, coaches, teachers, administrators, politicians, athletes, artists, etc.  There are also great examples of leaders portrayed in literature from whom we can learn about the complexity, responsibility, and opportunities that accepting a leadership role can provide.  In this course, students will read about leaders, self-select leaders of interest for further study, participate in student-led discussions, write analytical essays based on readings, and build a repertoire of leadership strategies to use in their everyday lives.

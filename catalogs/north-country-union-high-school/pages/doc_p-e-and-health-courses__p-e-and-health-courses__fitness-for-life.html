Course Title Fitness for Life

Grade Levels Open to grades   9-12 

Course Length: semester

Prerequisites: 

Course Description 

This course is designed for students who wish to learn and/or continue their fitness education.  Students participating in fitness for life will learn how to safely perform a variety of fitness skills including but not limited to, strength, endurance, balance, agility, and cardiovascular endurance.  Students will also learn the importance of nutrition and rest/relaxation.  Students are required to create their own personal journal that shows the student’s efforts to reach their fitness goals.

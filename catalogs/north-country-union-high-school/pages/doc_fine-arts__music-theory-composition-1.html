Course Title Music Theory & Composition 1

Grade Levels 10-12

Course Length: semester

Prerequisites Basic music theory

Course Description 

This class is offered to students who already possess basic music theory skills and wish to take their studies to a higher level.  Intensive work in music theory is combined with a course of study in music composition.  Students will compose a variety of pieces within specified guidelines, demonstrating knowledge of music notation and music elements. Enroll limit:  8

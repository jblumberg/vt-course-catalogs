Course Title: Coding with Math 1

Grade Levels: 9-12

Course Length: Full Year

Prerequisites: successful completion of Algebra 1 or Geometry preferred, but not required.

Course Description: Students explore fundamental properties of mathematics in algebra, geometry, and statistics through coding. Students will design, develop, implement, maintain, and manage software programs using a variety of platforms. Students become familiar with the many ways that computers can be used to simulate and model mathematical concepts using computer programming.

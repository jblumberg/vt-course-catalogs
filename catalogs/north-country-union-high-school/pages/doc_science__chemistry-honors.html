Course Title Chemistry Honors

Grade Levels 10-12

Course Length: Year (semester based)

Prerequisites  Must have received a 2.5 or better in Algebra 1 and must pass a math assessment.

Course Description 

This course will run as two, semester-long courses to offer flexibility in student scheduling.  Students signing up for Chemistry Honors should sign up for both semesters when registering for classes.  This course is similar to Chemistry but is designed and delivered in a manner that allows students to dive deeply into the mathematical relationships that are pervasive in the physical sciences.  This course includes extensive laboratory experience and requires students to use both writing & mathematical skills gained in the first two years of high school.  Students who plan to build their post-secondary education and career in STEM (science, technology, mathematics & engineering) fields should take this course.

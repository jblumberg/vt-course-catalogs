Course Title Instrumental Music Exploration

Grade Levels 9-12

Course Length: semester

Prerequisites none

Course Description 

This course is for any student who wants to learn to play an instrument.  The focus will be on “Band” instruments like trumpets, clarinets, and flutes but could include others.  The school owns many instruments that can be lent to students.  Students will practice individually and play music together as a group.  Students will learn basic music theory, including music-reading.  The type of music played will be built around the strength of the group as the semester moves forward.  This class could be taken by students who would like to play their first instrument OR students wanting to play a second instrument.

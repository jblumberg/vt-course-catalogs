Course Title AP Physics

Grade Levels 11-12

Course Length: Full Year

Prerequisites Must have successfully completed Algebra 1 and Geometry or teacher recommendation 

Course Description 

This course will be offered 2026-2027.  This class, which meets for 2 blocks, is an algebra based introductory college level physics course where students will be introduced to topics including Newtonian mechanics (including rotational motion); work, energy, and power. Students will spend at least 25% of the class time developing their understanding of these topics and inquiry skills through experimentation, including their ability to ask questions, develop models, plan and carry out investigation, analyze data, and draw conclusions based on this analysis.

Course Title: Junior Reserve Officer Training Class (J.R.O.T.C.)

Grade Levels: 9-12

Course Length: full year

Prerequisites: none

Course Description: The United States Army’s Junior Reserve Officers’ Training Corps (JROTC) is a character and leader development program that teaches transferable skills of ethics, citizenship, communication, leadership, responsibility, and how to serve the community.  The course teaches fundamental values, focus, and discipline that underpin successful college-level study and provides an edge for any career path while building a strong resume.  Voluntary after school activities include a Color Guard team, Marksmanship team, Raider challenge team and an Academic Bowl team.

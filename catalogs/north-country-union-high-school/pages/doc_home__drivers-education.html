Course Title: Driver’s Education

Grade Levels: 10-12

Course Length: semester

Prerequisites: must have driver’s permit & 10 hours driving w/Parent/Guardian

Course Description: Completing a drivers education course offers numerous benefits for students. First and foremost, it provides a solid foundation of knowledge and skills necessary for safe and responsible driving. Through this course, students learn about traffic laws, road signs, and defensive driving techniques. They also gain practical experience through behind-the-wheel training, which helps them develop confidence and competence behind the wheel. Apart from improving driving skills, drivers education can also lead to lower insurance rates for young drivers. Many insurance companies offer discounts to students who have successfully completed a drivers education program. Additionally, completing drivers education can enhance job prospects for students as it demonstrates their sense of responsibility and commitment to safety. Overall, drivers education equips students with the necessary knowledge and skills to become responsible and skilled drivers, while also providing potential financial and career benefits

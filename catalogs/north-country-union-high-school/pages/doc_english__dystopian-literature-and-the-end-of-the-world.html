Course Title: Dystopian Literature and the End of the World

Grade Levels: 11-12

Course Length: Semester

Prerequisites: None

Course Description: Dystopian Literature and the End of the World plunges students into the gripping worlds of modern dystopian and apocalyptic masterpieces. Students will explore the haunting landscapes of The Road, a collapsing America in Parable of the Sower, post-pandemic chaos in Station 11, and the politically charged dystopia of V for Vendetta. They will also read several short stories and poems from various authors and periods.  This course is reading and discussion intensive. Students will read 40-50 pages between classes and engage in discussions that will challenge perceptions and ignite the imagination. They will dissect the complexities of these novels and short stories, unraveling hidden meanings, and connecting the dots to our present reality. There will be several analytical papers/projects and a formal research paper. The course final is to craft a short dystopian story.

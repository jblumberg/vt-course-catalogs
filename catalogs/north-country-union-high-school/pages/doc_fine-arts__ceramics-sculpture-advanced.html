Course Title: Ceramics & Sculpture - Advanced

Grade Levels: 11 & 12

Course Length: Full Year

Prerequisites: Ceramics and Sculpture 1-Intro & Ceramics & Sculpture 2 - Intermediate

Course Description

This course is designed for the student who wishes to further pursue and investigate ceramic

and sculptural media. Building on knowledge and skills acquired in C&S1 and C&S2, students

will begin to target their talents and explore and develop their personal style as a 3D artist.  

Students will assess their own artwork and present a professional final display of their work

including artist statements. Teacher’s assessments will be based on student’s engagement

level, quality of design and construction, and professional feedback on final display.  This is a

dual enrollment course which earns each student 3 college credits!

Course Title Biology Honors

Grade Levels 9

Course Length: Year (semester based)

Prerequisites N/A

Course Description 

This course will run as two, semester-long courses to offer flexibility in student scheduling. Students signing up for Biology Honors should sign up for both semesters when registering for classes.  Topics covered are ecology (including a Clyde River field study), biochemistry, cellular biology, genetics and evolution.  This course involves extensive laboratory investigations and report writing.

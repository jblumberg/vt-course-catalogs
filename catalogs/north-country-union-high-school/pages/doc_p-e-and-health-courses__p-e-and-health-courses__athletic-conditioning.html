Course Title: Athletic Conditioning

Grade Levels: 10-12,  9th teacher permission

Course Length: semester

Prerequisites:  must have completed Fitness for Life and/or Fitness for Life for Women or teacher permission.

Course Description: Athletic Conditioning is recommended for all student athletes or students that are serious about improving their performance.  Students will learn and perform a variety of sound, safe and effective training techniques that will improve their strength, agility, quickness, speed, endurance, and balance.  Students will also learn the importance of nutrition, rest, and relaxation as to how it relates to both athletic performance and recovery.  Anatomy and physiology content will be incorporated to help students understand how and why these topics affect their lifestyle.  Students are required to create their own personal journal/manual as part of their class project.

Course Title:  AP French Language and Culture

Grade Levels: 11-12

Course Length: 1 year

Prerequisites:  Advanced French, or French III and teacher recommendation

Course Description: This course is designed for students who plan to take the AP French Language Examination.  It is for advanced students to develop the ability to understand spoken French in both formal and conversational situations; to acquire sufficient vocabulary skills for reading of newspaper and magazine articles,

contemporary literature and other non-technical writings; to express ideas accurately and resourcefully.  There is also emphasis on discussion of contemporary issues. The course is weighted and students are expected to take the A.P. test.  All French courses are discussion-based classes and verbal participation is mandatory.

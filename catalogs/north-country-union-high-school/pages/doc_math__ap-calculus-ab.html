Course Title AP Calculus AB

Grade Levels 10-12

Course Length: Full Year

Prerequisites Successful completion of Precalculus or teacher recommendation.

Course Description 

AP Calculus AB is equivalent to a first semester college calculus class and is designed to prepare you for the Advanced Placement Exam in May.  The two major concepts of calculus, differentiation of functions and integration, focus on instantaneous rate of change and cumulative change. Students who are enrolled in the course are expected to take the AB Advanced Placement test.

Course Title Beginning Guitar

Grade Levels 9-12

Course Length: semester

Prerequisites

Course Description 

Beginning Guitar is a one semester class designed to provide the musical novice with the fundamental guitar skills needed to perform at an introductory level.  Students will learn basic music skills and guitar techniques.  Classroom time will be spent learning to read music and guitar tablature while playing traditional melodies and chords.  Recommended for beginners or students who play guitar “by ear,” and would like to learn to read music.  Prior musical background is not necessary.

Course Title: Adventure Physical Education-1st Sem. (NCAC)   (see also NCAC section)

Grade Levels: 11-12 

Course Length: Semester

Prerequisites: Students must already be an intermediate skier/snowboarder and try out during Outdoor Club outings.

Course Description: This course provides high-level whitewater kayaking and mountain biking education for juniors and seniors either enrolled in the Career Center or at the High School.  Certified instructors provide training in cold water rivers and lakes.  Students will learn how to exit their boats safely and some will learn to roll their kayaks.  Students will learn uphill mountain biking techniques and downhill mountain biking techniques.   The Adventure Student will develop skills in leadership, problem solving, communication, risk management, and teamwork.  This program is extremely rigorous and designed for only the most adventurous individual.  This course runs in tandem with Adventure English to create a two-block experience entitled the North Country Adventure Course.

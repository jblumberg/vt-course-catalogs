Course Title Arts Integrated U.S. History Honors (ACA)

Grade Levels 10-11

Course Length: 1 year

Prerequisites 

Course Description 

Arts Integrated U.S. History Honors is the most challenging ACA Social Studies option for students. This course is strongly recommended for students considering Advanced Placement U.S. History or Advanced Placement European History in grade 12.  Using a chronological framework, a thematic approach and an arts focus, this course begins with the growth of the United States from the American Civil War and Southern Reconstruction through to the conclusion of the Cold War.  The course is designed to follow the major themes, ideas, politics and historical events in US history while making connections to its current realities in order to understand the effect history has had on our everyday life.  Instruction will focus on American history, literature and fine arts related to the history curriculum as well as a review of writing, research, and basic study skills in addition to essays and research papers.  Through the study of history through the lens of the arts from, and about, the historical periods and concepts that we are studying, we will make connections across these curricula to demonstrate how history is chronicled in the visual and performing arts of its time.  This course will include additional writing and reading assignments outside of class.  Writing assignments concentrate on essays of all types as well as the research paper.

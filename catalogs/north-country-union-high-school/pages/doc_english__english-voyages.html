Course Title:  English Voyages

Grade Levels: 11- 12

Course Length:  Year-Long or Semester

Prerequisites:  No

Course Description: English Voyages provides students with the opportunity to strengthen their reading and writing skills in a supportive and accommodating learning environment.  Working collaboratively, students will delve deep into young adult fiction, rehearse new vocabulary, and explore what it means to be a contributing member of society.  All work is done in class, so attendance is especially important. English Voyages is offered every year with a different curriculum, so students may enroll in it over multiple years.

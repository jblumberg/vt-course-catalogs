Course Title:  English 10 World Literature Honors

Grade Levels: 10

Course Length:  Year-Long

Prerequisites:  No

Course Description: This course is designed for students with a serious interest in the study of literature.  Students will demonstrate proficiency in reading and analyzing literature and in expressing themselves clearly in speech and writing.  The course will include critical reading and analysis of poetry, short stories, novels, drama, and nonfiction.  Emphasis will be on the close reading and intensive study of selected literary works, with a focus on World Literature.  Students will consider cultural conflicts, as well as the effects of modernization, political struggle, and other themes common in literary works.  Vocabulary and grammar development will be stressed throughout the year.  In addition to Scott Foresman’s World Literature anthology, major works to be studied may include, but are not limited to, In the Time of the Butterflies, Sold, Cry, the Beloved Country, a work by Shakespeare, and Arthurian Legends.  (Students must be willing to complete homework. Students must also be able to read and write fluently and independently to be successful in this class.)
